Privacy Policy

Govexamprep is a free exam-preparation website by Infinity Digital, and PrepFlight is its Android quiz app. This page explains what happens to your information.

  • No sign-up and no account. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email to use the website or the app.
  • Your scores, bookmarks and progress are saved only on your own device (in the browser or app storage). They are not sent to us.
  • The website itself uses no cookies and no tracking code.
  • There are no advertisements.
  • There are no in-app purchases, and the app does not ask for any payment.
  • Feedback is sent from your own email app. We receive only what you choose to write, and we use it only to reply and to improve the content.
  • We never ask for payment, card or bank details.
  • When you open a business link from our website, your browser tells that business only that the visit came from our website address. We do not share anything else about you.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with anyone.
  • You can clear all saved progress at any time by clearing the browser's or app's storage.
