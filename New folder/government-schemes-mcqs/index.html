Sample Government Schemes questions with answers

Q1. Swachh Bharat Mission was launched on:

स्वच्छ भारत मिशन कब शुरू किया गया?

  1. 15 August 2014
  2. 2 October 2014
  3. 26 January 2015
  4. 1 July 2015
Answer: 2 October 2014. Swachh Bharat Mission was launched on 2 October 2014, Gandhi Jayanti.
स्वच्छ भारत मिशन 2 अक्टूबर 2014 (गांधी जयंती) को शुरू किया गया।

Q2. In which year was the Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana launched?

प्रधानमंत्री उज्ज्वला योजना किस वर्ष शुरू की गई?

  1. 2015
  2. 2016
  3. 2017
  4. 2014
Answer: 2016. The Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana was launched in 2016.
प्रधानमंत्री उज्ज्वला योजना 2016 में शुरू हुई।

Q3. What is the main aim of the Saubhagya (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana)?

सौभाग्य (प्रधानमंत्री सहज बिजली हर घर योजना) का मुख्य उद्देश्य क्या है?

  1. Developing theme-based tourist circuits
  2. Electricity connection to every household
  3. Low-cost life insurance
  4. Direct income support to farmer families
Answer: Electricity connection to every household. Saubhagya (Pradhan Mantri Sahaj Bijli Har Ghar Yojana) (2017): electricity connection to every household.
सौभाग्य (प्रधानमंत्री सहज बिजली हर घर योजना) (2017): हर घर को बिजली कनेक्शन।

Q4. The Beti Bachao Beti Padhao scheme was launched in 2015 from:

बेटी बचाओ बेटी पढ़ाओ योजना 2015 में कहाँ से शुरू हुई?

  1. Panipat (Haryana)
  2. Ballia (Uttar Pradesh)
  3. Jaipur (Rajasthan)
  4. Varanasi (Uttar Pradesh)
Answer: Panipat (Haryana). It was launched at Panipat on 22 January 2015.
यह 22 जनवरी 2015 को पानीपत से शुरू हुई।

Q5. In which year was the Stand-Up India launched?

स्टैंड-अप इंडिया किस वर्ष शुरू की गई?

  1. 2014
  2. 2015
  3. 2016
  4. 2017
Answer: 2016. The Stand-Up India was launched in 2016.
स्टैंड-अप इंडिया 2016 में शुरू हुई।

Q6. What is the main aim of the MGNREGA?

मनरेगा (MGNREGA) का मुख्य उद्देश्य क्या है?

  1. Support for new startup businesses
  2. Guaranteed wage employment in rural areas
  3. Health insurance cover for poor families
  4. Preserving the heritage character of cities
Answer: Guaranteed wage employment in rural areas. MGNREGA (2006): guaranteed wage employment in rural areas.
मनरेगा (MGNREGA) (2006): ग्रामीण क्षेत्रों में गारंटीशुदा मज़दूरी रोज़गार।

Q7. What is the main aim of the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana?

प्रधानमंत्री फ़सल बीमा योजना का मुख्य उद्देश्य क्या है?

  1. Developing modern, citizen-friendly cities
  2. Preserving the heritage character of cities
  3. Crop insurance for farmers
  4. Small working-capital loans for street vendors
Answer: Crop insurance for farmers. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (2016): crop insurance for farmers.
प्रधानमंत्री फ़सल बीमा योजना (2016): किसानों के लिए फ़सल बीमा।

Q8. What is the main aim of the Swadesh Darshan?

स्वदेश दर्शन का मुख्य उद्देश्य क्या है?

  1. Developing modern, citizen-friendly cities
  2. Low-cost accident insurance
  3. Developing theme-based tourist circuits
  4. Short-term recruitment of 'Agniveers' into the armed forces
Answer: Developing theme-based tourist circuits. Swadesh Darshan (2015): developing theme-based tourist circuits.
स्वदेश दर्शन (2015): थीम आधारित पर्यटन सर्किट का विकास।

Q9. In which year was the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana launched?

प्रधानमंत्री सुरक्षा बीमा योजना किस वर्ष शुरू की गई?

  1. 2014
  2. 2016
  3. 2015
  4. 2013
Answer: 2015. The Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana was launched in 2015.
प्रधानमंत्री सुरक्षा बीमा योजना 2015 में शुरू हुई।

Q10. What is the main aim of the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana?

प्रधानमंत्री मुद्रा योजना का मुख्य उद्देश्य क्या है?

  1. Developing theme-based tourist circuits
  2. Loans for small and micro businesses
  3. Digital services and internet access for citizens
  4. Skill training for youth
Answer: Loans for small and micro businesses. Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (2015): loans for small and micro businesses.
प्रधानमंत्री मुद्रा योजना (2015): छोटे और सूक्ष्म व्यवसायों को ऋण।
