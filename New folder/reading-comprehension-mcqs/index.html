Sample Reading Comprehension questions with answers

Q1. Passage: Water is one of the most precious natural resources. Although three-fourths of the Earth is covered with water, only a small part of it is fresh water that we can use. Rivers, lakes and underground water supply most of our needs. Today many cities face water shortage because of a growing population, wastage and pollution. Simple habits such as closing taps, reusing water and collecting rainwater can help save water for the future. Which part of the Earth's water can we mainly use?

अनुच्छेद पढ़कर उत्तर दें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Passage: Water is one of the most precious natural resources. Although three-fourths of the Earth is covered with water, only a small part of it is fresh water that we can use. Rivers, lakes and underground water supply most of our needs. Today many cities face water shortage because of a growing population, wastage and pollution. Simple habits such as closing taps, reusing water and collecting rainwater can help save water for the future. Which part of the Earth's water can we mainly use?

  1. Only rainwater
  2. All the water
  3. Sea water
  4. A small part that is fresh water
Answer: A small part that is fresh water. The passage says only a small part is usable fresh water.
उत्तर अनुच्छेद में दी गई जानकारी पर आधारित है: The passage says only a small part is usable fresh water.

Ramesh was doubtful, but he tried it on one small field. To his surprise, that field needed less than half the water and gave a better crop. Now his whole farm uses drip irrigation, and his neighbours come to learn from him. What was the result on the field where he first tried it?

What was the result on the field where he first tried it?

  1. The pipes broke
  2. It used double the water

Answer: It used less than half the water and gave a better crop. That field needed less than half the water and gave a better crop.

Q4. Passage: Our village had no library until a retired teacher, Mr. Joshi, decided to change that. He emptied one room of his house and filled its shelves with his own books. He invited children to come every evening after school. At first only three children came. Soon word spread, and villagers began donating books and old newspapers. Within a year, more than fifty children were visiting regularly, and some adults also came to read. The panchayat was so impressed that it gave a proper building for the library and named it after Mr. Joshi. What does 'word spread' mean in the passage?

अनुच्छेद पढ़कर उत्तर दें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Passage: Our village had no library until a retired teacher, Mr. Joshi, decided to change that. The panchayat was so impressed that it gave a proper building for the library and named it after Mr. Joshi. What does 'word spread' mean in the passage?

  1. The children stopped coming
  2. Books were sent to other villages
  3. Words were written on walls
  4. More people came to know about it
Answer: More people came to know about it. 'Word spread' means news reached more people.
उत्तर अनुच्छेद में दी गई जानकारी पर आधारित है: 'Word spread' means news reached more people.

Q5. Passage: In the final over of the inter-school cricket match, our team needed six runs to win. Our best batsman, Kabir, hit the ball high, and it looked like a sure six. He said, 'You played with courage. Remember how that fielder never gave up on the ball. That is the lesson to take home.' Next year, we won the trophy. What lesson did the coach want the team to learn?

अनुच्छेद पढ़कर उत्तर दें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Passage: In the final over of the inter-school cricket match, our team needed six runs to win. Next, she learned to hold her breath and float with a board. After two months, she could swim the full length of the pool. At the end of the year, she won a medal in the school swimming competition. What did the coach advise Asha?

अनुच्छेद पढ़कर उत्तर दें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Passage: Asha was afraid of water. When her school announced swimming lessons, she pretended to be ill on the first day. Question: What is rainwater harvesting?
