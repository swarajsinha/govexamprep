Q1. Arrange the parts P, Q, R and S to make a meaningful sentence. The old man ... P. in the park Q. every morning. R. who lives next door S. goes for a long walk

भागों P, Q, R और S को सही क्रम में लगाकर सार्थक वाक्य बनाइए। The old man ... P. in the park Q. every morning. R. who lives next door S. goes for a long walk

  1. RSPQ
  2. QSPR
  3. PQSR
  4. SQRP
Answer: RSPQ. Correct sentence: The old man who lives next door goes for a long walk in the park every morning.
सही वाक्य: The old man who lives next door goes for a long walk in the park every morning.
