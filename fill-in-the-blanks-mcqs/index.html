Sample Fill in the Blanks questions with answers

Q1. She ___ to school every day.

She ___ to school every day.

  1. go
  2. goes
  3. going
  4. gone
Answer: goes. With a singular subject (she) in the simple present tense, the verb takes -s/-es: 'goes'.
Simple present tense में एकवचन कर्ता (she) के साथ क्रिया में -s/-es लगता है: 'goes'

Q2. How ___ water do you drink daily?

रिक्त स्थान भरें: How ___ water do you drink daily?

  1. many
  2. much
  3. a few
  4. few
Answer: much. 'Much' is used with uncountable nouns.
अगणनीय संज्ञा के साथ 'much' आता है।

Q3. The new hospital will be built on this ___.

रिक्त स्थान भरें: The new hospital will be built on this ___.

  1. site
  2. sight
  3. side
  4. cite
Answer: site. 'Site' = a place; 'sight' = vision; 'cite' = quote.
'Site' = स्थान; 'sight' = दृष्टि; 'cite' = उद्धृत करना।

Q4. The doctor advised him to ___ from smoking.

रिक्त स्थान भरें: The doctor advised him to ___ from smoking.

  1. refrain
  2. remove
  3. restrict
  4. refuse
Answer: refrain. 'Refrain from' means to stop yourself from doing something.
'Refrain from' = किसी काम से स्वयं को रोकना।

Q5. The farmers are ___ for rain after the long dry spell.

रिक्त स्थान भरें: The farmers are ___ for rain after the long dry spell.

  1. preying
  2. praying
  3. playing
  4. paying
Answer: praying. 'Pray' means to ask God earnestly.
'Pray' = प्रार्थना करना।

Q6. The two countries signed a ___ to end the war.

रिक्त स्थान भरें: The two countries signed a ___ to end the war.

  1. treasure
  2. treatment
  3. treaty
  4. trait
Answer: treaty. 'Treaty' means a formal agreement between countries.
'Treaty' = संधि।

Q7. A good leader must be ___ to the needs of the people.

रिक्त स्थान भरें: A good leader must be ___ to the needs of the people.

  1. sensual
  2. sentimental
  3. sensible
  4. sensitive
Answer: sensitive. 'Sensitive to' means aware of and responsive to.
'Sensitive to' = के प्रति संवेदनशील।

Q8. Please ___ attention to what the teacher says.

रिक्त स्थान भरें: Please ___ attention to what the teacher says.

  1. make
  2. do
  3. give off
  4. pay
Answer: pay. We say 'pay attention'.
सही प्रयोग 'pay attention' है।

Q9. Animals ___ themselves to their surroundings.

रिक्त स्थान भरें: Animals ___ themselves to their surroundings.

  1. admit
  2. adapt
  3. adept
  4. adopt
Answer: adapt. 'Adapt' means to adjust to new conditions.
'Adapt' = अनुकूल बनाना।

Q10. She ___ the job offer because the salary was low.

रिक्त स्थान भरें: She ___ the job offer because the salary was low.

  1. turned down
  2. turned in
  3. turned on
  4. turned up
Answer: turned down. 'Turn down' means reject.
'Turn down' = अस्वीकार करना।
