Sample Vocabulary questions with answers

Q1. Meaning of 'Diligent':

'Diligent' का अर्थ:

  1. Lazy
  2. Hard-working
  3. Rude
  4. Clever
Answer: Hard-working. Diligent means hard-working and careful.
Diligent = मेहनती

Q2. What is the meaning of the word 'Prolific'?

शब्द 'Prolific' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Producing a lot of work or results
  2. Wicked or criminal
  3. False; not genuine
  4. Friendly and easy to talk to
Answer: Producing a lot of work or results. 'Prolific' means: producing a lot of work or results.
'Prolific' का अर्थ है: विपुल, बहुत अधिक रचने वाला।

Q3. What is the meaning of the word 'Debilitate'?

शब्द 'Debilitate' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Likely to change suddenly and unpredictably
  2. Easily controlled; obedient
  3. To put off to a later time
  4. To make weak
Answer: To make weak. 'Debilitate' means: to make weak.
'Debilitate' का अर्थ है: कमज़ोर कर देना।

Q4. What is the meaning of the word 'Intrepid'?

शब्द 'Intrepid' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Fearless
  2. Very rich, expensive or generous
  3. Deserving blame
  4. Done secretly
Answer: Fearless. 'Intrepid' means: fearless.
'Intrepid' का अर्थ है: निडर।

Q5. What is the meaning of the word 'Sagacious'?

शब्द 'Sagacious' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Mercy shown in punishing
  2. To recognise or notice clearly
  3. Very unwilling to spend money
  4. Wise and shrewd
Answer: Wise and shrewd. 'Sagacious' means: wise and shrewd.
'Sagacious' का अर्थ है: बुद्धिमान, दूरदर्शी।

Q6. What is the meaning of the word 'Impetuous'?

शब्द 'Impetuous' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Bitter, long-lasting resentment
  2. Talking too much about unimportant things
  3. Acting quickly without thinking
  4. Anxious or fearful about the future
Answer: Acting quickly without thinking. 'Impetuous' means: acting quickly without thinking.
'Impetuous' का अर्थ है: उतावला।

Q7. What is the meaning of the word 'Feasible'?

शब्द 'Feasible' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Lacking self-confidence
  2. Very strict
  3. Possible to do easily
  4. Doubtful about something
Answer: Possible to do easily. 'Feasible' means: possible to do easily.
'Feasible' का अर्थ है: संभव, व्यवहार्य।

Q8. What is the meaning of the word 'Incessant'?

शब्द 'Incessant' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Continuing without stopping
  2. No longer existing or working
  3. Friendly and easy to talk to
  4. Gentle and not harmful
Answer: Continuing without stopping. 'Incessant' means: continuing without stopping.
'Incessant' का अर्थ है: निरंतर, लगातार।

Q9. What is the meaning of the word 'Hackneyed'?

शब्द 'Hackneyed' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Used so often that it is no longer interesting
  2. Suggesting that something bad will happen
  3. Intended to teach a moral lesson
  4. Holding firmly to something; determined
Answer: Used so often that it is no longer interesting. 'Hackneyed' means: used so often that it is no longer interesting.
'Hackneyed' का अर्थ है: घिसा-पिटा।

Q10. What is the meaning of the word 'Deplete'?

शब्द 'Deplete' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Intended to teach a moral lesson
  2. Showing great care and steady effort
  3. To use up a supply of something
  4. Selfless concern for others
Answer: To use up a supply of something. 'Deplete' means: to use up a supply of something.
'Deplete' का अर्थ है: भंडार घटा देना।
