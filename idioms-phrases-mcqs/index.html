Sample Idioms & Phrases questions with answers

Q1. What does the idiom 'a piece of cake' mean?

मुहावरे 'a piece of cake' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Something very easy
  2. Something tasty
  3. A small reward
  4. A difficult task
Answer: Something very easy. 'A piece of cake' means something that is very easy to do.
'A piece of cake' का अर्थ है ऐसा काम जो करने में बहुत आसान हो।

Q2. What is the meaning of the idiom 'up to the mark'?

मुहावरे 'up to the mark' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To be defeated or killed
  2. A cause of quarrel
  3. To praise oneself
  4. Of the required standard
Answer: Of the required standard. 'up to the mark' means: of the required standard.
'up to the mark' का अर्थ है: अपेक्षित स्तर का।

Q3. What is the meaning of the idiom 'to turn over a new leaf'?

मुहावरे 'to turn over a new leaf' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To bribe someone
  2. Very closely associated, especially in wrongdoing
  3. To change one's behaviour for the better
  4. To be kept waiting
Answer: To change one's behaviour for the better. 'to turn over a new leaf' means: to change one's behaviour for the better.
'to turn over a new leaf' का अर्थ है: सुधरकर नया जीवन शुरू करना।

Q4. What is the meaning of the idiom 'to take the bull by the horns'?

मुहावरे 'to take the bull by the horns' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To do things in the wrong order
  2. To face a difficulty boldly
  3. To fail or succeed entirely by one's own efforts
  4. To exaggerate a small problem
Answer: To face a difficulty boldly. 'to take the bull by the horns' means: to face a difficulty boldly.
'to take the bull by the horns' का अर्थ है: साहस से कठिनाई का सामना करना।

Q5. What is the meaning of the idiom 'to bell the cat'?

मुहावरे 'to bell the cat' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. A controversial issue that is difficult to handle
  2. To take a firm stand
  3. To change one's behaviour for the better
  4. To undertake a dangerous task for the good of others
Answer: To undertake a dangerous task for the good of others. 'to bell the cat' means: to undertake a dangerous task for the good of others.
'to bell the cat' का अर्थ है: दूसरों के लिए जोखिम भरा काम करना।

Q6. What is the meaning of the idiom 'to wash one's dirty linen in public'?

मुहावरे 'to wash one's dirty linen in public' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. An enemy who pretends to be a friend
  2. To be completely confused
  3. To undertake a dangerous task for the good of others
  4. To discuss private quarrels in public
Answer: To discuss private quarrels in public. 'to wash one's dirty linen in public' means: to discuss private quarrels in public.
'to wash one's dirty linen in public' का अर्थ है: घर की बात बाहर कहना।

Q7. What is the meaning of the idiom 'to hit below the belt'?

मुहावरे 'to hit below the belt' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To take a decision that cannot be reversed
  2. Fixed and settled in advance
  3. To act unfairly
  4. To be a common trait in a family
Answer: To act unfairly. 'to hit below the belt' means: to act unfairly.
'to hit below the belt' का अर्थ है: अनुचित तरीक़े से वार करना।

Q8. What is the meaning of the idiom 'to die in harness'?

मुहावरे 'to die in harness' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To die while still working
  2. A controversial issue that is difficult to handle
  3. To make the best use of an opportunity
  4. To understand the hidden meaning
Answer: To die while still working. 'to die in harness' means: to die while still working.
'to die in harness' का अर्थ है: काम करते-करते मरना।

Q9. What is the meaning of the idiom 'at the drop of a hat'?

मुहावरे 'at the drop of a hat' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To exhaust oneself by working too hard
  2. Immediately, without hesitation
  3. To praise oneself
  4. To shift responsibility to someone else
Answer: Immediately, without hesitation. 'at the drop of a hat' means: immediately, without hesitation.
'at the drop of a hat' का अर्थ है: तुरंत, बिना हिचक।

Q10. What is the meaning of the idiom 'to pay through the nose'?

मुहावरे 'to pay through the nose' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To be defeated or killed
  2. To pay too high a price
  3. To attract the most attention and praise
  4. A scholar or literary person
Answer: To pay too high a price. 'to pay through the nose' means: to pay too high a price.
'to pay through the nose' का अर्थ है: बहुत अधिक क़ीमत चुकाना।
