Sample Mathematical Operations questions with answers

Q1. If '+' means '−', '−' means '÷', '×' means '+', '÷' means '×', what is the value of 11 ÷ 10 × 16 × 9?

यदि '+' का अर्थ '−', '−' का अर्थ '÷', '×' का अर्थ '+', '÷' का अर्थ '×' हो, तो 11 ÷ 10 × 16 × 9 का मान क्या है?

  1. 132
  2. 135
  3. 139
  4. 142
Answer: 135. Replace the signs: 11 × 10 + 16 + 9 = 135 (apply BODMAS).
चिह्न बदलें: 11 × 10 + 16 + 9 = 135 (BODMAS लगाएँ)

Q2. If '+' means '−', '−' means '+', '×' means '÷', '÷' means '×', what is the value of 25 − 30 ÷ 23 × 30?

  1. 52
  2. 45
  3. 96
  4. 48
Answer: 48. After replacing the signs: 25 + 30 × 23 ÷ 30 = 48 (BODMAS).
चिह्न बदलने के बाद: 25 + 30 × 23 ÷ 30 = 48 (BODMAS)
