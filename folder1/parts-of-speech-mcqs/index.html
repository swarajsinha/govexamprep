Sample Parts of Speech questions with answers

Q1. Identify the part of speech of the word in stars: She sings *beautifully*.

स्टार वाले शब्द का शब्द-भेद पहचानें: She sings *beautifully*.

  1. Noun
  2. Adjective
  3. Adverb
  4. Verb
Answer: Adverb. 'Beautifully' describes how she sings, so it is an adverb.
'Beautifully' बताता है कि वह कैसे गाती है, अतः यह adverb (क्रिया-विशेषण) है।

Q2. Choose the correct word: The boy hurt ___ while playing.

सही शब्द चुनें: The boy hurt ___ while playing.

  1. himself
  2. him
  3. he
  4. his
Answer: himself. A reflexive pronoun is used when the action returns to the subject.
जब क्रिया का प्रभाव कर्ता पर ही लौटे, तब reflexive pronoun (himself) आता है।

Q3. Choose the abstract noun formed from the adjective 'deep'.

विशेषण 'deep' से बनी भाववाचक संज्ञा (abstract noun) चुनें।

  1. Deeply
  2. Deeper
  3. Deepen
  4. Depth
Answer: Depth. The abstract noun of 'deep' is 'depth'.
'deep' की भाववाचक संज्ञा 'depth' है।

Q4. In 'He always comes on time', the word 'always' is an adverb of:

वाक्य 'He always comes on time' में 'always' किस प्रकार का क्रिया-विशेषण है?

  1. Manner
  2. Frequency
  3. Degree
  4. Place
Answer: Frequency. It tells how often.
यह बताता है कि कितनी बार, अतः आवृत्तिवाचक (frequency) है।

Q5. Choose the correct collective noun: a ___ of ships

सही समूहवाचक संज्ञा (collective noun) चुनें: a ___ of ships

  1. Bouquet
  2. Choir
  3. Crew
  4. Fleet
Answer: Fleet. We say 'a fleet of ships'.
सही प्रयोग है 'a fleet of ships'।

Q6. Choose the correct word: The murderer was ___ at dawn.

सही शब्द चुनें: The murderer was ___ at dawn.

  1. hang
  2. hung
  3. hanged
  4. hunged
Answer: hanged. For execution, 'hang' becomes 'hanged'; for objects, it becomes 'hung'.
फाँसी के अर्थ में 'hanged' आता है; वस्तुओं को टाँगने के लिए 'hung'।

Q7. Choose the correct word: My brother is taller than ___.

सही शब्द चुनें: My brother is taller than ___.

  1. I
  2. me
  3. mine
  4. myself
Answer: I. In formal English the sentence means 'taller than I am', so the subject form 'I' is used.
औपचारिक अंग्रेज़ी में इसका अर्थ 'taller than I am' है, इसलिए कर्ता रूप 'I' आता है।

Q8. Choose the correct word: Let you and ___ finish this work together.

सही शब्द चुनें: Let you and ___ finish this work together.

  1. I
  2. myself
  3. mine
  4. me
Answer: me. 'Let' is followed by the object form of the pronoun.
'Let' के बाद सर्वनाम का कर्म रूप (me) आता है।

Q9. What is the correct plural of 'ox'?

'ox' का सही बहुवचन (plural) क्या है?

  1. Oxens
  2. Oxen
  3. Oxes
  4. Oxs
Answer: Oxen. The plural of 'ox' is 'oxen'.
'ox' का बहुवचन 'oxen' है।

Q10. Choose the correct collective noun: a ___ of wolves

सही समूहवाचक संज्ञा (collective noun) चुनें: a ___ of wolves

  1. Crew
  2. Swarm
  3. Bouquet
  4. Pack
Answer: Pack. We say 'a pack of wolves'.
सही प्रयोग है 'a pack of wolves'।
