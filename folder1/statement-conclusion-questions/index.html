Sample Statement & Conclusion questions with answers

Q1. Statement: All students who passed the exam got a certificate. Ravi got a certificate. Conclusions: I. Ravi passed the exam. II. Ravi did not appear in the exam.

कथन: परीक्षा पास करने वाले सभी छात्रों को प्रमाणपत्र मिला। रवि को प्रमाणपत्र मिला। निष्कर्ष: I. रवि ने परीक्षा पास की। II. रवि परीक्षा में नहीं बैठा।

  1. Only I follows
  2. Only II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Answer: Neither I nor II follows. The statement does not say only those who passed got certificates, so neither conclusion definitely follows.
कथन यह नहीं कहता कि केवल पास होने वालों को ही प्रमाणपत्र मिला, अतः कोई भी निष्कर्ष निश्चित नहीं।

  1. Only I follows
  2. Only II follows
  3. Both I and II follow
  4. Neither I nor II follows
Answer: Both I and II follow. Conclusion I follows and conclusion II follows in every possible Venn diagram.
