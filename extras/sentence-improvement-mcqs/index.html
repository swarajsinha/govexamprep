Sample Sentence Improvement questions with answers

Q1. Improve the part in stars: He *did not knew* the answer.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: He *did not knew* the answer.

  1. did not know
  2. does not knew
  3. did not known
  4. No improvement
Answer: did not know. After 'did not', use the base form 'know'.
'Did not' के बाद मूल रूप 'know' लगता है।

Q2. Improve the part in stars: If she *will study* hard, she will pass.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: If she *will study* hard, she will pass.

  1. would study
  2. had studied
  3. studies
  4. No improvement
Answer: studies. First conditional: if + simple present, will + verb.
पहला conditional: if + simple present, will + क्रिया।

Q3. Improve the part in stars: Neither the coach nor the players *was* happy.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: Neither the coach nor the players *was* happy.

  1. has been
  2. is
  3. were
  4. No improvement
Answer: were. With 'neither … nor', the verb agrees with the nearer subject.
'neither … nor' में क्रिया पास वाले कर्ता के अनुसार आती है।

Q4. Improve the part in stars: He has *two sister-in-laws*.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: He has *two sister-in-laws*.

  1. two sister-in-law
  2. two sisters-in-law
  3. two sisters-in-laws
  4. No improvement
Answer: two sisters-in-law. The main word takes the plural: sisters-in-law.
मुख्य शब्द बहुवचन होता है: sisters-in-law।

Q5. Improve the part in stars: She *herself* cooked the whole meal.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: She *herself* cooked the whole meal.

  1. her
  2. hers
  3. she
  4. No improvement
Answer: No improvement. The sentence is correct: 'herself' is used for emphasis.
वाक्य सही है: 'herself' ज़ोर देने के लिए है।

Q6. Improve the part in stars: He is *a* honest officer.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: He is *a* honest officer.

  1. the
  2. no article
  3. an
  4. No improvement
Answer: an. 'Honest' has a silent h.
'Honest' में h मौन है।

Q7. Improve the part in stars: He insisted *to go* there alone.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: He insisted *to go* there alone.

  1. for going
  2. at going
  3. on going
  4. No improvement
Answer: on going. 'Insist' takes 'on' + -ing.
'Insist' के साथ 'on' + -ing आता है।

Q8. Improve the part in stars: The murderer was *hung* last year.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: The murderer was *hung* last year.

  1. hanged
  2. hang
  3. hunged
  4. No improvement
Answer: hanged. For execution use 'hanged'.
फाँसी के अर्थ में 'hanged' आता है।

Q9. Improve the part in stars: Neither Ram *or* Shyam was present.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: Neither Ram *or* Shyam was present.

  1. and
  2. but
  3. nor
  4. No improvement
Answer: nor. 'Neither' pairs with 'nor'.
'Neither' के साथ 'nor' आता है।

Q10. Improve the part in stars: I have *much* friends in Pune.

स्टार वाले भाग को सुधारें: I have *much* friends in Pune.

  1. a much
  2. many
  3. much more
  4. No improvement
Answer: many. 'Friends' is countable, so 'many'.
'Friends' गणनीय है, इसलिए 'many'।
