Idioms & Phrases (मुहावरे) – उत्तर सहित नमूना प्रश्न

प्र1. 'To be in hot water' का अर्थ:

  1. बहुत खुश होना
  2. स्नान करना
  3. गर्मी लगना
  4. मुसीबत में होना
उत्तर: मुसीबत में होना। मुसीबत में होना।

प्र2. मुहावरे 'to draw the line' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To catch someone in the act of doing wrong
  2. To set a limit on what one will accept
  3. Likely to happen
  4. To take a decision that cannot be reversed
उत्तर: To set a limit on what one will accept। 'to draw the line' का अर्थ है: सीमा तय करना।

प्र3. मुहावरे 'to bring to light' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. An unbelievable, made-up story
  2. Very close in a race or contest
  3. To reveal or make known
  4. To attract the most attention and praise
उत्तर: To reveal or make known। 'to bring to light' का अर्थ है: प्रकट करना।

प्र4. मुहावरे 'with flying colours' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To follow someone's example
  2. With great success
  3. To suspect that something is wrong
  4. To fail to produce the expected effect
उत्तर: With great success। 'with flying colours' का अर्थ है: शानदार सफलता के साथ।

प्र5. मुहावरे 'to read between the lines' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Of the required standard
  2. To understand the hidden meaning
  3. To make the best use of an opportunity
  4. To go to bed
उत्तर: To understand the hidden meaning। 'to read between the lines' का अर्थ है: छिपा हुआ अर्थ समझना।

प्र6. मुहावरे 'to lend an ear' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To remain calm in a difficult situation
  2. To listen carefully
  3. To finish the hardest part of a task
  4. To keep changing one's opinion
उत्तर: To listen carefully। 'to lend an ear' का अर्थ है: ध्यान से सुनना।

प्र7. मुहावरे 'to cross the Rubicon' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To tease someone in a friendly way
  2. A memorable and important day
  3. To take a decision that cannot be reversed
  4. False sorrow
उत्तर: To take a decision that cannot be reversed। 'to cross the Rubicon' का अर्थ है: ऐसा निर्णय लेना जिससे लौटा न जा सके।

प्र8. मुहावरे 'to throw in the towel' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. To admit defeat
  2. A person who feels uncomfortable in unfamiliar surroundings
  3. In the same difficult situation
  4. To be ruined
उत्तर: To admit defeat। 'to throw in the towel' का अर्थ है: हार मान लेना।

प्र9. मुहावरे 'to go the extra mile' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. Deliberately and cruelly
  2. To admit one's mistake and apologise humbly
  3. To make more effort than is expected
  4. To face the consequences of one's actions
उत्तर: To make more effort than is expected। 'to go the extra mile' का अर्थ है: अपेक्षा से अधिक प्रयास करना।

प्र10. मुहावरे 'the apple of one's eye' का अर्थ क्या है?

  1. A person who is loved very dearly
  2. To be defeated or killed
  3. To discuss private quarrels in public
  4. To make neither profit nor loss
उत्तर: A person who is loved very dearly। 'the apple of one's eye' का अर्थ है: आँखों का तारा।
