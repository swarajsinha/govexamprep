Sample Books & Authors questions with answers

Q1. Who wrote 'Gitanjali'?

'गीतांजलि' किसने लिखी?

  1. Premchand
  2. Rabindranath Tagore
  3. Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay
  4. Sarojini Naidu
Answer: Rabindranath Tagore. Rabindranath Tagore wrote Gitanjali and received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913.
रवींद्रनाथ टैगोर ने गीतांजलि लिखी और 1913 में साहित्य का नोबेल पुरस्कार प्राप्त किया।

Q2. Who wrote 'Train to Pakistan'?

'ट्रेन टू पाकिस्तान' किसने लिखी?

  1. Bhisham Sahni
  2. Khushwant Singh
  3. Kuldip Nayar
  4. Amrita Pritam
Answer: Khushwant Singh. 'Train to Pakistan' was written by Khushwant Singh.
'ट्रेन टू पाकिस्तान' खुशवंत सिंह की रचना है।

Q3. Who wrote 'Hind Swaraj'?

  4. Vinoba Bhave
Answer: Mahatma Gandhi. 'Hind Swaraj' was written by Mahatma Gandhi.
'हिंद स्वराज' महात्मा गांधी की रचना है।

Q7. Who wrote 'Pallav'?

'पल्लव' किसकी रचना है?

  1. Amrita Pritam
  2. Kamleshwar

Q8. Who wrote 'A Passage to India'?

'अ पैसेज टू इंडिया' किसकी रचना है?
