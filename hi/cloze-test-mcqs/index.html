छोटे नोट्स पढ़ेंव्याख्या के साथ अभ्यास10 प्रश्नों की क्विज़समयबद्ध टॉपिक टेस्ट

Cloze Test – उत्तर सहित नमूना प्रश्न

प्र1. रिक्त स्थान भरें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Cloze passage: Trees are our best friends. They (6) ___ us oxygen. Birds build their nests (7) ___ trees. Cutting trees (8) ___ the environment. Therefore, we must plant (9) ___ trees and take care (10) ___ them. रिक्त स्थान (9).

  1. more
  2. few
  3. much
  4. less
उत्तर: more। 'More trees' fits the meaning.

प्र2. रिक्त स्थान भरें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Cloze passage: The village had no proper road, (1) ___ the children had to walk through mud during the rains. The villagers wrote (2) ___ the district officer many times. At last a road (3) ___ built last year. Now buses run regularly, and farmers can take (4) ___ crops to the market easily. Life in the village has become much (5) ___. Fill blank (5).

  1. more easier
  2. easiest
  3. easier
  4. easy
उत्तर: easier। 'Much' + comparative: 'much easier'।

प्र3. रिक्त स्थान भरें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Cloze passage: Yoga (1) ___ practised in India for thousands of years. It helps to keep the body fit (2) ___ the mind calm. Today people all over the world (3) ___ yoga. International Yoga Day is celebrated (4) ___ 21 June every year. Even a few minutes of yoga daily can (5) ___ our health. Fill blank (3).

  1. practises
  2. practised to
  3. practise
  4. practising
उत्तर: practise। 'People' बहुवचन है: 'practise'।

प्र4. रिक्त स्थान भरें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Cloze passage: Ravi was (1) ___ intelligent boy, but he was very lazy. He never did his homework (2) ___ time. One day his teacher told him that talent without effort (3) ___ useless. Ravi took the advice seriously. He started (4) ___ hard, and by the end of the year he had become one of the best students (5) ___ the class. Fill blank (2).

  1. by
  2. in
  3. on
  4. at
उत्तर: on। 'On time' = ठीक समय पर।

प्र5. रिक्त स्थान भरें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Cloze passage: Mount Everest is the highest peak (1) ___ the world. Many climbers try to reach (2) ___ top every year. The climb is very (3) ___ because of the cold, strong winds and lack of oxygen. Climbers must train for months (4) ___ they attempt it. Only those with strong will and good health (5) ___ succeed. Fill blank (5).

  1. could have
  2. can
  3. has
  4. must have
उत्तर: can। 'Can succeed' सामान्य क्षमता दिखाता है।

प्र6. रिक्त स्थान भरें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Cloze passage: Last Sunday our class went on a trip (1) ___ the zoo. We reached there (2) ___ ten o'clock. First we saw the lions, which (3) ___ sleeping in the shade. Then a guide told us many interesting facts (4) ___ elephants. It was (5) ___ most enjoyable day of the term. Fill blank (5).

  1. an
  2. more
  3. the
  4. a
उत्तर: the। Superlative ('most enjoyable') के साथ 'the' आता है।

प्र7. रिक्त स्थान भरें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Cloze passage: Newspapers play an important role (1) ___ a democracy. They inform people about events happening around (2) ___. They also point out the mistakes of the government and (3) ___ public opinion. However, newspapers must report facts honestly and avoid (4) ___ false news. A free and fair press is essential (5) ___ a healthy democracy. Fill blank (2).

  1. theirs
  2. they
  3. their
  4. them
उत्तर: them। 'around' के बाद कर्म रूप 'them'।

प्र8. रिक्त स्थान भरें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Cloze passage: Yoga (1) ___ practised in India for thousands of years. It helps to keep the body fit (2) ___ the mind calm. Today people all over the world (3) ___ yoga. International Yoga Day is celebrated (4) ___ 21 June every year. Even a few minutes of yoga daily can (5) ___ our health. Fill blank (5).

  1. improvement
  2. improves
  3. improve
  4. improving
उत्तर: improve। 'can' के बाद मूल रूप आता है।

प्र9. रिक्त स्थान भरें (अनुच्छेद अंग्रेज़ी में है): Cloze passage: The library is a quiet place (1) ___ people go to read and study. It has thousands of books (2) ___ many subjects. Students can borrow books and must return them (3) ___ two weeks. Talking loudly is not (4) ___ inside. A library is a treasure house (5) ___ knowledge. Fill blank (2).

  1. at
  2. on
  3. for
  4. in to
उत्तर: on। 'Books on many subjects' — 'on' = के बारे में।

प्र10. निम्नलिखित गद्यांश में रिक्त स्थान (3) के लिए सबसे उपयुक्त विकल्प चुनें। Trees are very (1) ______ for our planet. They give us oxygen and (2) ______ the air clean. Their roots hold the soil (3) ______ that it is not washed away by rain.

  1. but
  2. so
  3. because
  4. or
उत्तर: so। 'so that' उद्देश्य बताता है: जड़ें मिट्टी को इसलिए पकड़ती हैं ताकि वह बह न जाए।
