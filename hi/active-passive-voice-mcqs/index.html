Active & Passive Voice (वाच्य) – उत्तर सहित नमूना प्रश्न

प्र1. Passive voice में बदलें: Ram paints the wall.

  1. The wall was painted by Ram.
  2. The wall is being painted by Ram.
  3. The wall is painted by Ram.
  4. The wall has been painted by Ram.
उत्तर: The wall is painted by Ram.। simple present में passive की संरचना: कर्म + is/am/are + V3 + by + कर्ता। अतः: The wall is painted by Ram.

प्र2. Passive voice में बदलें: He was repairing the car.

  1. The car was being repaired by him.
  2. The car is being repaired by him.
  3. The car was repaired by him.
  4. The car had been repaired by him.
उत्तर: The car was being repaired by him.। past continuous में passive की संरचना: कर्म + was/were + being + V3 + by + कर्ता। अतः: The car was being repaired by him.

प्र3. Passive voice में बदलें: They offered him a job.

  1. He is offered a job by them.
  2. Him was offered a job by them.
  3. He was offered a job by them.
  4. He has been offered a job by them.
उत्तर: He was offered a job by them.। व्यक्ति (him → he) कर्ता बनता है; simple past → was + V3।

प्र4. Passive voice में बदलें: She has helped us.

  1. We have been helped by her.
  2. We had been helped by her.
  3. We were helped by her.
  4. We are helped by her.
उत्तर: We have been helped by her.। present perfect में passive की संरचना: कर्म + has/have + been + V3 + by + कर्ता। अतः: We have been helped by her.

प्र5. Passive voice में बदलें: They do not respect him.

  1. He was not respected by them.
  2. He is not respected by them.
  3. He does not respected by them.
  4. He has not been respected by them.
उत्तर: He is not respected by them.। नकारात्मक वाक्य के passive में 'not' पहली सहायक क्रिया के बाद आता है।

प्र6. Passive voice में बदलें: Ram is painting the wall.

  1. The wall has been painted by Ram.
  2. The wall is painted by Ram.
  3. The wall is being painted by Ram.
  4. The wall was being painted by Ram.
उत्तर: The wall is being painted by Ram.। present continuous में passive की संरचना: कर्म + is/am/are + being + V3 + by + कर्ता। अतः: The wall is being painted by Ram.

प्र7. Passive voice में बदलें: My father drives a truck.

  1. A truck was driven by my father.
  2. A truck is driven by my father.
  3. A truck is being driven by my father.
  4. A truck has been driven by my father.
उत्तर: A truck is driven by my father.। simple present में passive की संरचना: कर्म + is/am/are + V3 + by + कर्ता। अतः: A truck is driven by my father.

प्र8. वह विकल्प चुनें जो दिए गए वाक्य को कर्मवाच्य (passive voice) में व्यक्त करता है। The teacher praised the students.

  1. The students had been praised by the teacher.
  2. The students are praised by the teacher.
  3. The teacher was praised by the students.
  4. The students were praised by the teacher.
उत्तर: The students were praised by the teacher.। Simple past (active) → passive में 'was/were + past participle'।

प्र9. वह विकल्प चुनें जो दिए गए वाक्य को कर्मवाच्य (passive voice) में व्यक्त करता है। My mother painted the house.

  1. The house had been painted by my mother.
  2. The house was being painted by my mother.
  3. The house was painted by my mother.
  4. The house is painted by my mother.
उत्तर: The house was painted by my mother.। नियम: simple past → was/were + past participle। कर्म, कर्ता बन जाता है: The house was painted by my mother.

प्र10. वह विकल्प चुनें जो दिए गए वाक्य को कर्मवाच्य (passive voice) में व्यक्त करता है। Ravi has painted the house.

  1. The house had been painted by Ravi.
  2. The house have been painted by Ravi.
  3. The house is painted by Ravi.
  4. The house has been painted by Ravi.
उत्तर: The house has been painted by Ravi.। नियम: present perfect → has/have been + past participle। कर्म, कर्ता बन जाता है: The house has been painted by Ravi.
