Fill in the Blanks (रिक्त स्थान) – उत्तर सहित नमूना प्रश्न

प्र1. रिक्त स्थान भरें: He was tired, ___ he went to bed early.

  1. but
  2. because
  3. although
  4. so
उत्तर: so। 'So' परिणाम बताता है।

प्र2. रिक्त स्थान भरें: She is intelligent; ___, she is very hardworking.

  1. however
  2. unless
  3. otherwise
  4. moreover
उत्तर: moreover। 'Moreover' = इसके अलावा।

प्र3. रिक्त स्थान भरें: Many Indians ___ to Canada every year.

  1. immerse
  2. emerge
  3. emigrate
  4. immigrate
उत्तर: emigrate। 'Emigrate' = अपना देश छोड़कर दूसरे देश जाना।

प्र4. रिक्त स्थान भरें: He promised to help but ___ at the last moment.

  1. backed out
  2. backed in
  3. backed into
  4. backed up
उत्तर: backed out। 'Back out' = वादे से मुकर जाना।

प्र5. रिक्त स्थान भरें: Our car ___ on the highway.

  1. broke out
  2. broke in
  3. broke down
  4. broke off
उत्तर: broke down। 'Break down' = ख़राब हो जाना।

प्र6. रिक्त स्थान भरें: Dark clouds showed that a storm was ___.

  1. eminent
  2. imminent
  3. emigrant
  4. immense
उत्तर: imminent। 'Imminent' = आसन्न।

प्र7. रिक्त स्थान भरें: Do you have ___ questions?

  1. some of
  2. any
  3. a little
  4. much
उत्तर: any। प्रश्न में 'any' आता है।

प्र8. रिक्त स्थान भरें: The boy was ___ for breaking the window.

  1. published
  2. pushed
  3. punished
  4. polished
उत्तर: punished। 'Punish' = दंड देना।

प्र9. रिक्त स्थान भरें: I cannot ___ his rude behaviour any longer.

  1. put up with
  2. put down to
  3. put off with
  4. put up to
उत्तर: put up with। 'Put up with' = सहन करना।

प्र10. रिक्त स्थान भरें: The manager ___ my request for leave.

  1. graded
  2. guarded
  3. grabbed
  4. granted
उत्तर: granted। 'Grant' = स्वीकृत करना।
