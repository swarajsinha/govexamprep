Sample Active & Passive Voice questions with answers

Q1. Change into passive voice: She writes a letter.

Passive voice में बदलें: She writes a letter.

  1. A letter was written by her.
  2. A letter is written by her.
  3. A letter has been written by her.
  4. A letter is being written by her.
Answer: A letter is written by her.. Simple present active → 'is/am/are + past participle'. So: A letter is written by her.
Simple present active → 'is/am/are + past participle'। अतः: A letter is written by her.

Q2. Change into passive voice: They were praising her.

Passive voice में बदलें: They were praising her.

  1. She was praised by them.
  2. She is being praised by them.
  3. She had been praised by them.
  4. She was being praised by them.
Answer: She was being praised by them.. In the past continuous tense, the passive is: object + was/were + being + V3 + by + agent. So: She was being praised by them.
past continuous में passive की संरचना: कर्म + was/were + being + V3 + by + कर्ता। अतः: She was being praised by them.

Q9. Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice. They are building a bridge.

वह विकल्प चुनें जो दिए गए वाक्य को कर्मवाच्य (passive voice) में व्यक्त करता है। They are building a bridge.

  1. A bridge was being built by them.
  2. A bridge is being built by them.
  3. A bridge has been built by them.
  4. A bridge is built by them.
Answer: A bridge is being built by them.. Rule: present continuous → is/are being + past participle. The object becomes the subject: A bridge is being built by them.
नियम: present continuous → is/are being + past participle। कर्म, कर्ता बन जाता है: A bridge is being built by them.
